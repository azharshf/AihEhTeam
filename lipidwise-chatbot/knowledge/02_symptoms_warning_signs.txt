SYMPTOMS AND WARNING SIGNS OF DYSLIPIDEMIA

Dyslipidemia usually has no obvious symptoms. This is why screening is important.

Possible signs when lipid levels are very high include:
- Yellowish patches around the eyelids (xanthelasma).
- Small yellow, red, or waxy bumps on the skin (xanthomas).
- Fatty lumps over tendons, especially near the Achilles tendon.
- A white or gray ring around the colored part of the eye (arcus cornealis).
- Tingling or burning feelings in the hands or feet, especially with very high triglycerides.

Major warning signs usually come from complications:
- Chest pain or shortness of breath (possible heart attack).
- Stroke-like symptoms such as face drooping, arm weakness, or trouble speaking.
- Leg pain when walking due to poor blood flow (peripheral artery disease).
- Severe upper abdominal pain, nausea, or vomiting from possible pancreatitis.

EMERGENCY SYMPTOMS: If a user reports chest pain, stroke-like symptoms (face drooping, arm weakness, trouble speaking), severe abdominal pain, or severe shortness of breath, they should seek emergency medical care immediately. These are life-threatening warning signs.
